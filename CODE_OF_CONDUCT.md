# Code of conduct

Pofyland is a small project with a friendly sea. Keep it that way.

## Our pledge

We want taking part here (issues, discussions, pull requests) to be a harassment-free experience for everyone,
whatever their age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected

- Be kind and assume good intent; many people here write in their second language.
- Give and take feedback about the work, not the person.
- Say thanks, admit mistakes, and help newcomers.

## Not OK

- Insults, harassment, or sexualized language and imagery
- Personal or political attacks, trolling
- Publishing someone's private information without permission
- Anything you would not say to a colleague face to face

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may block people
who keep doing it. Report a problem privately to the maintainer through their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/konuksev)
or, for anything sensitive, through a private [security report](https://github.com/konuksev/Pofyland/security/advisories/new).
Reports are kept confidential.

Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.
