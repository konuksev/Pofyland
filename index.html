Pofyland

Your AI agent sessions as jellyfish in a calm sea on your Mac.

Pofyland shows your Claude Code, Codex and Antigravity sessions as live jellyfish. Working ones swim; the ones waiting on you come to the glass. Approve from your Mac or your phone. Everything runs locally.

PoflowGrowth

PoflowGrowth is the small publishing tool we use to share Pofyland updates on our own social accounts (X, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, TikTok). It drafts posts from our releases and videos, and publishes them only after we approve each one.
