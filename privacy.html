Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

This policy covers the Pofyland Mac app and PoflowGrowth, the publishing tool we use for Pofyland's own social media accounts.

Pofyland app

Pofyland runs on your Mac. It reads the status of AI coding sessions on that Mac to draw them on screen. Session data stays on your devices; we do not collect it, sell it or send it to our servers. If you pair your phone, approvals travel between your own devices.

PoflowGrowth

PoflowGrowth connects to social media accounts that we own (for example @pofyland on X, Instagram, TikTok and YouTube, and the Pofyland Facebook page) through each platform's official API. With the permissions granted by the account owner it can:

  • read basic profile information and post statistics of the connected account,
  • upload videos and publish posts that the account owner has reviewed and approved.

Access tokens are stored only on the computer that runs PoflowGrowth, in a file readable by its user. Drafts, approvals and statistics are kept in a local database on that computer. We do not share this data with third parties, use it for advertising, or access accounts we do not own.

Platform data

Content published through PoflowGrowth is also subject to the privacy policy of the platform it is published on (X, Meta, Google/YouTube, TikTok). PoflowGrowth's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

An account owner can revoke PoflowGrowth's access at any time from the platform's connected apps settings. Deleting the local token file and database removes all data PoflowGrowth holds.
